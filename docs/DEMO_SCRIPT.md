# Two-minute demo video script

Record the screen (app open in a browser) with your voice and a small webcam bubble. Use your own best slow-motion
drive, already analysed once so the model is warm. Words in *italics* are what you say.

**0:00 – 0:15 · Who and why**
*"I'm Muskan, a Senior AI Engineer with a physics background. For the past year I've been researching AI for elite
cricket batting biomechanics. Golf measures the kinematic sequence with K-Vest — but only in training. Nobody does it
for cricket batting. This is a zero-hardware first step."*

**0:15 – 0:35 · Input**
Show the upload box. *"One front-on phone clip, filmed from the bowler's end. No markers, no lab. Google MediaPipe
tracks the batter."* Press **Analyse**.

**0:35 – 1:05 · The hero: the sequence**
Show the summary card and the chart. *"Pelvis, torso, lead arm, hands — each should peak just after the one below it,
inside-out, and speed up the next. Here they fire in order with gaps of about thirty milliseconds."* Point at a link:
*"It also checks speed gain and braking — whether each segment slows down to pass energy up the chain. That's where
power leaks show up."*

**1:05 – 1:25 · Evidence and coaching**
Play a few seconds of the slow-motion replay with the live speed bars, then the evidence frames. Scroll to one work-on
card: *"Every insight has the same shape — what happened, why it matters, the evidence moment, and what to try."*

**1:25 – 1:45 · Honesty and engineering**
Open the Honesty box. *"These are estimates from a single 2D video, and the app says so. Depth comes from a learned
model, so I refine it with a rigid-bone constraint — a miniature digital twin — and I report bone-length wobble as a
tracking-quality metric. On synthetic swings with known timing it recovers the sequence reliably at 60 fps and above;
at 30 fps it's marginal, and the app warns you."*

**1:45 – 2:00 · The vision and the ask**
*"The full system adds a lab-built digital twin per player, multi-angle high-speed and event cameras, force plates,
eye tracking and bat sensors — so it measures each player's own timing chain in live matches, not positions against a
generic ideal. I'd love to build this with Google's AI cricket coach team."* End on the summary card and the Space link.

**Recording tips:** 1080p, browser zoom 90 %, hide bookmarks, practise twice, keep it under 2:00.
