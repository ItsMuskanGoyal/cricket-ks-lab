# Digital twin lite: what to measure and where to enter it

## Where to enter it
In the app, left column, open **"Digital twin lite - the player's body measurements (optional)"** (just above the
**Analyse the swing** button). Leave the profile on "None", type the numbers into the five boxes, then press
**Analyse the swing**. Measure the person **in the video**.

**Height alone is enough.** If you only fill in Height, the app estimates the limbs from standard body
proportions. The four limb lengths make it more precise.

## How to measure
Use a soft tailor's tape, in **centimetres**, standing relaxed, arms hanging by the sides. A friend makes it much
easier. Measure the **front (lead) side**: the left side for a right-handed batter. Measure each one twice and use
the average.

| Box in the app | From | To | How to find the points |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Height (cm)** | floor | top of head | Barefoot, back against a wall, book flat on the head, mark the wall. |
| **Upper arm: shoulder → elbow** | bony tip on the top-outside of the shoulder | bony point on the outside of the elbow | Run your fingers along the top of the shoulder until they hit the bony edge. For the elbow, bend the arm slightly and feel the bump on the outer side. |
| **Forearm: elbow → wrist** | same elbow bony point | middle of the wrist, at the crease | Measure along the outside (thumb side) of the forearm. |
| **Thigh: hip → knee** | the bony bump on the side of the upper thigh | middle of the side of the knee (the joint line) | Lift the knee a little and the hip bump moves under your fingers - that's the point. The knee joint line is the soft gap between thigh bone and shin bone. |
| **Shin: knee → ankle** | knee joint line (side of the knee) | the outer ankle bone (the bump) | Measure down the outside of the leg. |

Typical adult values, to sanity-check yours: upper arm ≈ 18–19 % of height, forearm ≈ 14–15 %, thigh and shin
≈ 24–25 % each. For a 160 cm person that's about 29, 23, 39 and 39 cm.

## What it changes
- Puts distances (stride, head movement) and hand speed on the player's real scale.
- The **bone-length wobble** tile shows how steadily each bone was tracked; it is reported with or without
  measurements.
- Rotation speeds and the firing order do **not** depend on these numbers.
